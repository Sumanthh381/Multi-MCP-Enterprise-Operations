# Alarm response and rationalization philosophy
Critical alarms require immediate assessment by a qualified operator. The demonstration operator response efficiency KPI measures the fraction of alarms acknowledged within 120 seconds. Acknowledgement does not prove resolution or safe operation.

Repeated alarms and long standing alarms are candidates for engineering rationalization. Review the alarm purpose, consequence, setpoint, deadband and delay. Never suppress a safety alarm solely because it recurs. Alarm flooding means at least ten events in ten minutes in this simulator. Correlation and co-occurrence do not establish a causal relationship.

Escalations should include the asset, time range, severity, observed alarm count, maintenance history, uncertainty and exact procedure citations. Obtain explicit human approval before creating a ticket or changing an operating condition.
