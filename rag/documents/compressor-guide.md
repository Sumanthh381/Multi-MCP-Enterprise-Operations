# Compressor and motor maintenance investigation
High vibration and bearing temperature alarms on compressors and motors require review of lubrication, shaft alignment and recent work orders. Compare event timestamps across related assets to identify a possible common utility disturbance. Treat correlations as leads for investigation rather than proof.

Verify instrument quality and maintenance records before an escalation. A critical condition must be assessed by the shift supervisor under the approved plant procedure. Do not restart equipment or bypass safety trips on the basis of document retrieval alone.
