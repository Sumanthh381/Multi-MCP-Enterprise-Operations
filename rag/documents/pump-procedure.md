# Feed pump vibration and suction troubleshooting
Recurring high vibration combined with low suction pressure on Boiler Feed Pump 101 or 102 can indicate suction strainer fouling, cavitation, or bearing misalignment. These are hypotheses requiring field verification, not a remote diagnosis.

Verify suction pressure against a calibrated instrument. Inspect the suction strainer differential pressure and available net positive suction head. Check bearing lubrication, temperature and alignment using approved isolation and lockout procedures.

For critical vibration or bearing temperature alarms, notify the shift supervisor immediately and follow the site trip procedure. Never bypass protective interlocks, automatically suppress alarms, or continue operation based solely on this advisory copilot. Record the alarm identifiers and maintenance findings in an escalation ticket after operator approval.
